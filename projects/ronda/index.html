Ronda

Shared expenses without the napkin maths.

Concept project: the brand, context and data are fictional. I made it to show how I design and build; it is not client work.

What I did
User experience, interface design and splitting logic
Built for
Friends, trips and shared flats
Made with
Astro · TypeScript · Vitest
Year
2026

Context

After the barbecue, the bill

On any group outing someone buys the meat, someone else the drinks, someone the charcoal. Then come the sums, which usually end in extra transfers, rounding someone quietly absorbs, or a “we’ll sort it later” that never happens.

Ronda is built for that moment: note who paid for what in seconds, and let the app say exactly how to get even.

Goal

Log fast
Add an expense in under ten seconds, on a phone, at the table.
Split fairly
Never lose or invent a peso, even when it does not divide evenly.
Settle easily
Suggest the fewest possible transfers.

Exploration

Experience decisions

  • One screen per question. “What was spent?” and “Where do we stand?” are two tabs, not one list that mixes everything.
  • The form slides up from the bottom, where the thumb is, and reads amounts the way people write them in Argentina: 12.500 or 980,50.
  • Balances read before you read them: bars go one way for people owed money and the other way for people who owe.
  • An identity that feels casual: bar-napkin yellow and a wide typeface. It does not look like a banking app, because it isn’t one.

Experience

Try it

Technical architecture

  • Amounts are stored as whole cents: when splitting, leftover cents are handed out one by one, so all balances always add up to zero.
  • Settling uses a greedy algorithm: the biggest debtor pays the biggest creditor, repeatedly. For a group of n people it needs at most n − 1 transfers.
  • Splitting, balances and the amount parser are covered by Vitest tests. The interface is framework-free TypeScript; the server renders the initial state.

Evaluation

Works
Adding expenses with validation, splitting between some people, balances and transfers.
Validated
Tests for splitting, settling and reading Argentine amounts.
Conceptual
Saved groups, user accounts, multiple currencies and built-in payments. The brand is fictional.
