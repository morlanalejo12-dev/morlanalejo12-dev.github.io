Termia

Cold-chain monitoring for refrigerated fleets.

Concept project: the brand, context and data are fictional. I made it to show how I design and build; it is not client work.

What I did
Product, interface design and prototype development
Built for
People who run fleets carrying refrigerated food or vaccines
Made with
Astro · TypeScript · SVG · Vitest
Year
2026

termia

TR-04 · 20 min > 8 °C

Context

The cold chain breaks quietly

A distributor moving refrigerated food or vaccines needs the load to stay between 2 and 8 °C. When a truck drifts out of that range (a door left open too long, a failing unit), the goods can be lost, and often nobody finds out until they arrive.

Termia is built for the person running that fleet: see at a glance which units are fine, which had trouble and what needs checking today.

Goal

Detect
Every out-of-range stretch, with its length and peak, without reading spreadsheets.
Prioritise
Show what needs a person first: excursions nobody has reviewed.
Close the loop
Reviewing an alert is an action, not a note to self.

Exploration

Design decisions

  • The safe range is part of the chart. The 2–8 °C band is always drawn and every excursion is marked on top of it, so the problem shows up before you read a number.
  • Three columns, three jobs: the fleet (where), the detail (what happened) and the alerts (what’s left to do). On small screens they stack in that order.
  • One signal colour. Orange only appears where something needs attention, and reviewing the alert turns it off on the unit.
  • Honest numbers. Every figure is computed from the series on screen. Nothing is decorative.

Experience

Try it

Technical architecture

What runs on this page is plain TypeScript and SVG: no chart library, no UI framework.

  • A seeded generator produces plausible, repeatable series: a random walk around 4.5 °C plus door-open events.
  • Excursion detection, summaries and chart paths are pure functions covered by Vitest tests.
  • The server (Astro) renders the first state, so the dashboard shows even if JavaScript fails; the browser then redraws with the same functions.

Evaluation

Works
Unit selection, 24 h or 6 h window, indicators, chart and the alert review flow.
Validated
Excursion and summary logic is covered by automated tests.
Conceptual
Real sensors, history, notifications and user accounts. Termia and its operator are fictional.
